# HelloSmart.ai Knowledge Base

**HelloSmart.ai is an AI-native hiring platform.** Companies assess candidates by having them do real work, with AI tools, inside a controlled workspace. Independent AI reviewer agents watch the work and score it against the company's own rubric. People make the final decision.

This knowledge base is the central manual to learn what the product is, who uses it, how every part works, and how it is sold and operated. Read it in order and you will understand the complete product lifecycle.

> **Version:** 0.2, MMP scope, 22 October 2026. Everything here comes from the product's business requirements workbook (`HelloSmart_Business_Requirements.xlsx`). Items that are not part of the first release are clearly marked **Phase 2** or **Phase 3**.

---

## The Product in 60 Seconds

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **What is it?** | An assessment platform where candidates do the real job with AI tools in sandboxed workspaces, evaluated with cited evidence. |
| **Category** | AI-native hiring platform (*"Hire by doing"*). |
| **Who buys it?** | Companies hiring for technical and non-technical niche roles: startups, staffing agencies, enterprises. |
| **Who uses it?** | Company Admins, Candidates, and HelloSmart Platform Admins. |
| **How is it sold?** | Sales-assisted: prospect books via cal.com, receives a quote, pays, and is provisioned on the multi-tenant route. No public self-signup in MMP. |
| **What makes it different?** | Works for any role, templates are strictly private to each company, rubrics run as independent AI agents, and humans stay in control. |
