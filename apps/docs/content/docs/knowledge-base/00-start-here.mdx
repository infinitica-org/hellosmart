**HelloSmart.ai is an AI-native hiring platform.** Companies assess candidates by having them do real work, with AI tools, inside a controlled workspace. Independent AI reviewer agents watch the work and score it against the company's own rubric. People make the final decision.

This knowledge base is the single place to learn what the product is, who uses it, how every part works, and how it is sold and operated. Read it in order and you will understand the whole product.

> **Version:** 0.2, MMP scope, 22 October 2026. Everything here comes from the product's business requirements workbook. Items that are not part of the first release are clearly marked **Phase 2** or **Phase 3**.

### The product in 60 seconds

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is it? | A platform where candidates do the real job, with AI, and get scored with evidence. |
| Category | AI-native work simulation for hiring ("Hire by doing"). |
| Who buys it? | Companies hiring for technical and non-technical niche roles. |
| Who uses it? | Company admins, candidates, and HelloSmart's own platform admins. |
| How is it sold? | Sales-assisted: book a meeting, get quoted, get provisioned. No public self-signup in the first release. |
| What makes it different? | It works for any role, tasks are private to each company, rubrics run as independent AI agents, and humans stay in control. |
