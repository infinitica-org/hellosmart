**Can a company sign up on its own?**
Not in the first release. A HelloSmart Platform Admin creates every company after a sales call. Self-serve will be revisited after the first 10 to 15 paying customers.

**Are templates shared between companies?**
No. Each template is assigned to specific companies, and no company can see another's. There is no public template catalog.

**Who pays for the AI candidates use?**
The company, through its own provider key, including during the trial. HelloSmart can also allocate a capped platform key as a quoted add-on. Self-serve managed credits come in Phase 2.

**Does the AI decide who gets hired?**
No. Reviewer agents produce scores with cited evidence. A person scores, overrides and finalizes, and the product never auto-rejects a candidate.

**Can candidates see their scores?**
Only if the company releases them. The default is off.

**What happens if a candidate leaves full screen?**
In Cloud mode the workspace disconnects, the event is logged, and the candidate can resume. Their work is saved. It is evidence for a reviewer, not an automatic failure.

**What if the candidate's internet drops?**
Work is saved continuously. They can reconnect until their time or window ends. The timer keeps running unless the assessment has "Pause timer on disconnect" turned on.

**What exactly is recorded?**
It depends on the mode and the consent the candidate gave: activity, the AI conversation and screen recording as configured in Cloud; the chat transcript in Chat. The candidate sees exactly what is recorded before they connect.

**How long is candidate data kept?**
By default 90 days after the assessment closes, configurable per company. Candidates and companies can request deletion.

**Which roles can be assessed?**
Any role the template and rubric can describe. In the first release Cloud VM covers technical roles and Chat covers non-technical roles. Browser-based roles come in Phase 2.

**Can candidates use their own computer?**
Local mode, Phase 2, allows it with a one-line command or small desktop agent, while AI calls still go through the proxy.

**What does a trial include?**
X templates, Y candidates and Z days, set by HelloSmart. Suggested defaults are 3, 10 and 14. A candidate counts once, at first workspace launch.

**What happens when the trial ends or a cap is reached?**
New assessments, candidates and workspace launches are blocked with a prompt to contact HelloSmart or pay. Running sessions finish and data stays readable.

**Can a company use its own domain or remove the HelloSmart mark?**
Yes, as paid add-ons: own routing for the domain and white label for the mark and branding.

**How do companies pay?**
Through an emailed payment link or inside the app, once or on a recurring plan, through Razorpay (cards, UPI, netbanking), with GST invoices. Stripe follows in Phase 2.

**Can a company edit a score after finalizing?**
Yes. Every edit creates a new version, and the original agent scores and earlier versions are kept.

**What if a workspace will not start?**
The system retries, then gives the candidate a support route and extra time automatically, and alerts HelloSmart.

**Is the product compliant with hiring-AI regulations?**
It is built around the principles those rules require: human oversight, logging, transparency and consent. Specific requirements by market, including the retention period, are to be confirmed with legal counsel.
